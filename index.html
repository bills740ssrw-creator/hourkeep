How it works

Four steps to a clean timesheet.

No onboarding call. No 40-minute setup video. Just this:

  1. Create a client or project

    Name a client, add a project with an hourly rate. Takes about thirty seconds.

  2. Start a timer or add time manually

    Tap start when work begins, stop when it ends — or log hours after the fact with date and duration.

  3. Review your hours

    Check daily, weekly and monthly totals, split by billable and non-billable, per client or project.

  4. Export or share a report

    Download a CSV for your records or to attach to an invoice. Done.

Privacy

A time tracker should track time. Nothing else.

HourKeep is a personal productivity tool, not employee surveillance software. Specifically:

— i

No screenshots

HourKeep never captures your screen. There is no screenshot feature to enable, disable or misconfigure.

— ii

No keystroke logging

What you type outside HourKeep is none of its business. It records only the time entries you create.

— iii

No hidden monitoring

No GPS tracking, no activity scores, no background surveillance. If a timer is running, you started it and you can see it.

Your entries are stored locally in your own browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere. Export or permanently delete everything anytime from Settings. Read the full Privacy Policy.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Who is HourKeep for?

Freelancers, consultants, small agencies and independent professionals who bill clients by the hour and want simple records without enterprise software.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The time tracker is free to use right now — unlimited clients, projects, entries and exports. Paid Pro and Team plans with cloud sync are coming soon.

Can I track billable and non-billable time?

Yes. Every entry can be marked billable or non-billable, and reports show the split side by side.

Can I use different currencies?

Yes. Amounts default to USD and you can switch to EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, NZD and other currencies in Settings. Formatting follows your locale.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Export reports as CSV, download a full JSON backup of everything, or permanently delete all your data from Settings at any time.

Does HourKeep monitor employees?

No. There are no screenshots, no keystroke logging, no GPS tracking and no hidden monitoring of any kind. It's a personal time tracker — a timer only runs when you start it.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The tracker is a responsive web app that works on phones, tablets and desktops, with a prominent start/stop button and touch-friendly forms.
