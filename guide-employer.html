Tutorial · If you review / invoice

Set rates once. Review weekly. Invoice with confidence.

2-minute readFor freelancers invoicing clients & small-agency owners

HourKeep today is a single-user, local-first tracker: data lives in your browser, there is no shared team dashboard yet. As an owner you use it to keep your own books clean — and to review hours your contractors send you as CSV exports. What it never does: screenshots, keystroke logging, GPS, or any hidden monitoring.

1 · Set up clients & rates (5 minutes, once)

  1. Open the tracker → Clients & projects tab.
  2. Press + New client for each paying client (e.g. “Acme Studio”).
  3. Under each client press + Project: name the work, set the hourly rate, mark billable or non-billable. Example: “Website redesign · $85/hr · billable”, “Internal admin · non-billable”.
  4. In Settings set your currency and time zone once. Amounts and day boundaries follow these everywhere.

Archive (don't delete) finished projects — they disappear from the timer but stay in history.

2 · The weekly review (10 minutes every Friday)

  1. Open Reports → This week.
  2. Check the four numbers: Total · Billable · Non-billable · Billable amount.
  3. Scan By client — does each client's hours match what you expect? Click into odd entries and fix them on the Tracker tab.
  4. Press Export CSV. That file is your source of truth: attach it to the invoice or paste totals into your accounting tool.

3 · Working with contractors (today's honest workflow)

  • Each person tracks their own hours in their own browser (send them the Employee guide).
  • On Friday each person sends you their Reports → Export CSV.
  • You reconcile: compare CSV totals against the project, then invoice the client from your own tracker.
  • What you don't get (yet): live team dashboards, approvals, or shared workspaces — those are planned Team-plan features, not current ones.
“If Friday review takes more than 10 minutes, your project list is too granular. Merge small tasks.”

4 · Invoicing checklist

  1. Reports → This month → filter mentally per client.
  2. Export CSV → keep one copy for your records.
  3. Invoice: client name, project lines with hours × rate, billable total. Non-billable stays internal.
  4. Settings → Export JSON monthly as a full backup. Data lives per-browser — backups are your safety net.

Rules that keep trust high

  • Bill what was agreed: only mark billable what the client agreed to pay for. Internal fixes and learning time stay non-billable.
  • Describe tasks like the client reads them: “Homepage wireframes v2 + feedback round” beats “stuff”.
  • No surveillance, ever: HourKeep cannot screenshot or score your team — and that is a selling point, not a gap. Say so in contracts.

Troubleshooting

  • Amounts look wrong? Check the project's hourly rate and the currency in Settings. Amounts = hours × rate, rounded once to the cent.
  • Hours on the wrong day? Check the time zone in Settings — reports group by day in that zone.
  • Need to wipe demo data? Sample entries are labeled “(example)” — delete them individually, or Settings → Delete all (exports first).
