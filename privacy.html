Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

HourKeep is a time-tracking website and browser-based tracker. This policy describes what data exists, where it lives, and what we don't collect. It is written for transparency — it will be reviewed by legal counsel before any paid plans launch.

Time-tracker data stays in your browser

Clients, projects, time entries, timer state and settings you create in the tracker are stored exclusively in your own browser's local storage. They are never uploaded to our servers, because there are no HourKeep servers that receive them. Clearing your browser storage removes them.

Waitlist email

If you join the waitlist, your email address is sent once via our form provider so we can notify you at launch. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or added to any other list.

What we never collect

  • Screenshots of your screen.
  • Keystrokes, mouse movements or activity scores.
  • GPS location or background tracking of any kind.
  • Contacts, files or other personal data from your device.

Analytics and cookies

HourKeep sets no cookies and runs no third-party analytics. The site loads fonts and images from third-party CDNs, which may log standard request data (such as IP addresses) under their own policies.

Your control

You can export your tracker data as CSV or JSON at any time, and permanently delete everything from the tracker's Settings screen. To ask about the waitlist email, contact us below.
