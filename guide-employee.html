Tutorial · If you log hours

Log your hours in 3 steps. Nothing watches you.

60-second readFor freelancers, contractors & team members

HourKeep is a personal timer, not surveillance software. No screenshots, no keystroke logging, no GPS. A timer only runs when you press Start, and every entry lives in your browser only.

1 · Start the timer when work begins

  1. Open the tracker → stay on the Tracker tab.
  2. Pick your Project (recent ones appear as quick chips).
  3. Type what you are doing — e.g. “Homepage wireframes v2”. This line shows up on invoices, so be specific.
  4. Check Billable is correct, then press ▶ Start timer (or press S on your keyboard).

You will see elapsed time ticking plus a live earnings estimate. Safe to refresh or close the tab — the timer uses timestamps, so it survives.

2 · Stop when you finish

  1. Press Stop & save entry (or S again).
  2. Your entry drops into Today's entries with hours + amount calculated to the cent.
  3. Made a mistake? Press Edit on the entry, fix it, save. Done.

3 · Forgot to start? Add it manually

  1. Open “Forgot to start the timer? Add time manually”.
  2. Enter date, start, end — watch the Duration preview (e.g. “2.50h · ≈ $212.50”).
  3. End earlier than start means next day (overnight shifts). Identical start/end is rejected — enter a real duration.

Daily & weekly habits

  • End of day: glance at the Today strip (hours · billable · entries). Anything missing → add manually while it is fresh.
  • End of week: open Reports → This week, check totals by client, Export CSV and send it with your invoice.
  • Filter: when Today gets long, type in the filter box to find one task fast.

What your manager sees (and doesn't)

HourKeep has no cloud sync yet — nobody can watch you live. Your manager only sees what you choose to send (a CSV export or invoice). Your keystrokes, screen and location are never recorded. Read the Privacy Policy.

“Start when work starts. Stop when it ends. Export on Friday. That is the whole job.”

Troubleshooting

  • Timer still running after refresh? Good — that is by design. Press Stop to save it.
  • Wrong project? Stop, Edit the entry, move it to the right project.
  • “Choose a project first”? You have no active projects — use the setup wizard or + New project next to the dropdown.
  • Data gone on a new device? Expected: data lives per-browser. Use Settings → Export JSON as backup before switching devices.
